## The new direction

The headline is **Who Are You Bringing With You?** The page connects Jesus' practice of bringing people close and entrusting real ministry to a practical examination of relationships. The concentric-circle graphic is removed. Three equal-width sections have large, equal-sized lining numerals and direct questions:

- **1 / Your own walk.** Before considering who you lead, how are you following Jesus?
- **3 / People you trust.** Who can pray with you, speak honestly, and see beyond the demands of ministry?
- **12 / People you're forming.** Who is learning beside you, and what are you helping them become ready to carry?

One shared Christ-centered field anchors all three sections. Both the Pastor and the people being formed remain disciples of Jesus, dependent on Him and the Holy Spirit. This is not a leadership chart with the Pastor taking Jesus' place.

Peter, James, and John belonged to the Twelve. The numbers are relational prompts, not ranks or required headcounts. Modern trusted support can also come from outside the church. Gethsemane illustrates Jesus' openness about His sorrow, not proof that the Three successfully supported Him or a command to select precisely three helpers.

The practical close asks: **Who could you invite closer? What could you begin entrusting to them?** It ends with one conversation, not a new program to staff.
