## Intentional changes from the proposed wording

- Preserve "Some Pastors are not losing their passion. They are losing the capacity to carry it."
- Make recognition tentative: "Grief and conflict can follow you home" and "you may be bracing for the next call," rather than assuming that every reader has those experiences.
- Replace the Pastor-facing final fire challenge with "You do not have to carry this alone." It is followed by "You can begin privately, with someone you trust."
- Retain "Do not send a wounded Pastor back into an unchanged fire" only in leader-facing delivery notes next to actual coverage and relief. This is not the last word addressed to a hurting Pastor.
- Keep three care responses unnumbered and concurrent, not a recovery ladder or imposed timeline: protect rest/coverage, bring trusted/professional care close, change harmful expectations.
- The light metaphor concerns human capacity, never diminished divine power, absence of Christ, or deficient faith. The notes make that explicit.
- Preserve Mark's fuller context: the returning apostles, Jesus' invitation to rest, the arriving crowd and His compassion. Keep 2 Timothy 1:6-7 with verse 8 in the notes; never turn it into "push harder."
- Separate public institutional questions from optional private conversations. Explain confidentiality limits, seek independent support if leaders contributed to harm, and do not collect trauma histories or treatment details.
- Give church leaders responsibility for relief, coverage, willingness/capacity/time/support, interruption handling, and operational follow-up. Do not transfer overload to Staff/Volunteers or require disclosure before relief.

## Visual copy as built

### When the Light Grows Dim

**Some Pastors are not losing their passion. They are losing the capacity to carry it.**

You can still love Jesus, love people, and believe deeply in your calling while being worn down by what you've carried.

Some things don't end when Sunday is over. Grief and conflict can follow you home. Even in quiet moments, you may be bracing for the next call.

**The Heartbeat**

When the disciples returned, Jesus heard what they had done and taught. They had not had time to eat. He invited them to come away and rest.

Mark 6:30-32 / paraphrase.

**Make room to recover.** Protect rest with someone else actually covering the work.

**Bring care close.** Make room for trusted support and qualified professional care.

**Change what keeps causing harm.** Share responsibility and change harmful expectations.

**You do not have to carry this alone.**

You can begin privately, with someone you trust.

A conversation about care, not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

## Sources and limits

- [Mark 6:30-34, NKJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+6%3A30-34&version=NKJV): biblical narrative; pastoral-care application is ours, not a clinical protocol.
- [Matthew 26:36-46, NIV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26%3A36-46&version=NIV): Jesus voiced sorrow and invited trusted disciples near; do not diagnose Him.
- [2 Timothy 1:6-8, NIV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Timothy+1%3A6-8&version=NIV): gift/power/love/self-discipline in costly faithfulness, not an instruction to work through injury.
- [WHO: Burn-out](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/frequently-asked-questions/burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon): occupational phenomenon, not a medical condition.
- [VA: PTSD Basics](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/understand/what/ptsd_basics.asp): symptoms do not establish diagnosis; qualified mental-health assessment needed. Burnout, grief, distress, and PTSD are not interchangeable.
- [988 Lifeline: What to Expect](https://988lifeline.org/get-help/what-to-expect/): U.S. call/text crisis support. For immediate danger use emergency services.

Sources checked September 16, 2026. No Barna statistic is used or inferred. The guide offers notice/support/referral and operational changes, not diagnosis, trauma processing, treatment, or a mandated return plan.
