# Scriptural Foundation

This is a working foundation. It can be refined as the mission and vision become clearer.

## Primary Scripture: 2 Timothy 1:6–7

The primary Scripture gathering this work together is **2 Timothy 1:6–7**.

Paul reminds Timothy to tend the gift God has already placed within him. Timothy is not told to manufacture a calling, create power through effort, or become someone else. He is reminded that what God has given him is to be nurtured and carried through the Holy Spirit—with power, love, and sound judgment rather than fear.

The surrounding passage matters:

- Paul remembers Timothy in prayer.
- Timothy's sincere faith has a history through Lois and Eunice.
- Paul personally reminds and encourages him.
- The gift is connected to relationship, calling, and entrusted responsibility.
- The power needed to remain faithful comes from God.
- The good deposit is guarded with the help of the Holy Spirit who lives within us.

This means the passage is not only about individual courage. It carries calling, spiritual empowerment, relationship, formation, perseverance, and faithful stewardship—the same threads running through this endeavor.

### Guardrail

“Fan into flame” must never become a way to tell an exhausted pastor to try harder, feel more passionate, or hide depletion. The gift comes from God. The power comes from the Holy Spirit. Love shapes how the gift is used. Sound judgment includes limits, wisdom, shared responsibility, and honest care.

Paul is not shouting motivation at Timothy from a distance. He is praying for him, remembering his tears, naming the faith already present in him, and reminding him what God has entrusted to him.

## Supporting Movement

These passages gather beneath the primary Scripture as one movement:

**Presence → Power → Rootedness → Direction → Completion → Perseverance**

### Presence: They are not doing this alone

- **Matthew 28:18–20** — Jesus sends His disciples with the promise of His continuing presence.
- **John 14:16–18** — Jesus promises the Spirit will remain with and live within His people; He will not leave them as orphans.

### Power: They are empowered by the Holy Spirit

- **Acts 1:8** — Power for witness comes through the Holy Spirit rather than personality, experience, or effort alone.
- **Zechariah 4:6** — God's work is accomplished through His Spirit rather than human strength.

### Rootedness: They are rooted in Christ

- **Colossians 2:6–7** — Those who have received Christ continue in Him, rooted, built up, and strengthened.
- **John 15:1–5** — Jesus is the vine and the source of lasting fruit. Apart from Him, effort and systems cannot create life.

### Direction: The Lord establishes their steps

- **Psalm 37:23–24** — The Lord establishes the steps of the person who delights in Him and upholds that person when he or she stumbles.
- **Proverbs 16:9** — We can plan faithfully while recognizing that the Lord establishes our steps.

### Completion: God continues the work He began

- **Philippians 1:3–6** — Confidence rests in God continuing His work. The surrounding verses connect that work with partnership in the gospel.

### Perseverance: We are surrounded by a great cloud of witnesses

- **Hebrews 11:39–12:3** — The testimony of faithful people encourages perseverance, but the passage directs our eyes toward Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith.

The witnesses never replace Jesus as the center. Their faithfulness reminds us that we are part of a story larger than ourselves and encourages us to keep running with our eyes fixed on Him.

## Working Expression

> **You are not carrying this alone. Christ is with you. The Holy Spirit empowers you. Your life and ministry remain rooted in Jesus. The Lord establishes your steps, continues the work He began, and surrounds you with the testimony and companionship of faithful people. So we keep moving forward with our eyes fixed on Jesus.**
