A picture of renewal

Rooted. Renewed. Multiplying Life.

Bare is not the same as dead. The roots may still be deep. The calling may still be alive. The people may still be faithful. When a church abides in Christ, gets clear about its calling, replaces draining systems with life-giving ones, and turns outward again, hidden life begins to break through. Buds become branches. Fruit begins to form. Seeds take root beyond the original tree.

1

Rooted

What looks dormant is not dead. The calling is still there. The roots are holding more life than anyone can see.

2

Awakening

Clarity gives that buried passion somewhere to go. Vision sharpens. Mission becomes clear. New life starts breaking through.

3

Flourishing

The church is no longer spending all its strength just surviving. Healthy systems make room for people to grow, serve, and breathe.

4

Fruitful

The church moves beyond its walls. Trust grows. Relationships deepen. The community begins to experience the fruit.

5

Multiplying

Life no longer stops at the original tree. Seeds take root. New leaders rise. New ministry grows. The impact reaches beyond a program, a building, or one personality.

Reading the tree

  • Roots are the calling, theology, values, and spiritual health that hold everything else.
  • Buds are the first visible signs that something hidden is waking up.
  • Flowers and pollinators show the church building real relationships with the community around it.
  • Fruit and seeds are life change that can travel beyond one moment or ministry.
  • Saplings are new life and new leadership growing beyond the original tree.

The Heartbeat

Systems do not create life. Jesus does. Healthy systems make room for the life He is already growing.

A healthy church is rooted in Christ, alive through the Holy Spirit, tended by the Father, and bearing fruit that reaches people with the saving grace of Jesus.
