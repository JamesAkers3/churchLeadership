The 1 • 3 • 12 principle

Christ at the Center

The Three remain inside the Twelve. The numbers show widening trust and responsibility—not rank, value, or importance.

12 • Wider Circle
3 • Trusted Few
1 • Lead Yourself
CHRISTThe Center
1 • Lead Yourself Under ChristLet Jesus lead you before you try to lead everyone else.
3 • Bring a Trusted Few CloseLet them know you, pray with you, tell you the truth, and help carry the weight.
12 • Form a Wider CircleInclude the three. Teach them, equip them, trust them, and give them real ministry.

At the center: Every relationship and every responsibility remains under Christ.
