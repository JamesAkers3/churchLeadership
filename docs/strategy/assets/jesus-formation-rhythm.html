A pattern we see in Jesus

The Way Jesus Formed People

He called them to be with Him. Then He taught them, trusted them, sent them, and welcomed them back.

CHRISTBe With Him
AbideStay close to Jesus
Share LifeLet trusted people know you
Teach & ModelShow them what faithfulness looks like
Equip & EntrustGive real responsibility and authority
Send TogetherSend with a partner and a clear assignment
Return & RestListen, correct, encourage, and breathe
MultiplyTrust the Holy Spirit for fruit beyond you

The Heartbeat: Being with Jesus comes before being sent by Jesus. This is a living rhythm, not a rigid ladder.
