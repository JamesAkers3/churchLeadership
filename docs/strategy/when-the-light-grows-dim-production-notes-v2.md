## Exact visual copy

CHRIST AT THE CENTER / PASTORAL CARE

### When the Light Grows Dim

**Some Pastors are not losing their passion.**

**They are losing the capacity to carry it.**

You may be the one everyone calls. The one who steps in when someone else can't.

It can be hard to receive care when you're used to being the one who gives it.

### You are not the only one.

**More than 6 in 10**

U.S. senior Protestant Pastors report feeling emotionally or mentally exhausted **frequently or sometimes.**

You can love Jesus and still be exhausted. You are not failing because you are hurting. Receiving care is not a failure of faith.

### The Heartbeat

When the disciples returned, Jesus heard what they had done and taught. They had not had time to eat. He invited them to come away and rest.

MARK 6:30-32 / PARAPHRASE

**Make room to recover.** Protect rest with someone else actually covering the work.

**Bring care close.** Offer trusted support and qualified professional care when needed.

**Change what keeps causing harm.** Share responsibility and change harmful expectations.

### You do not have to carry this alone.

You can begin privately, with someone you trust.

Source: Barna Group with Gloo, "Pastors' Sense of Calling Is Up - Satisfaction Is Lagging," May 5, 2026. 2026 survey: 507 U.S. senior Protestant Pastors. Self-reported exhaustion, not a diagnosis; reported exhaustion has declined over the past decade.

A conversation about care, not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

## Research and theological limits

Primary sources rechecked September 16, 2026:

- [Barna, May 5, 2026](https://www.barna.com/research/pastors-calling-satisfaction-research-2026/): reports "just over 60 percent" experiencing emotional or mental exhaustion frequently or sometimes. "More than 6 in 10" is an approximate restatement, not an invented exact percentage. 2026 n=507 U.S. senior Protestant Pastors; PastorPanel uses quotas and weighting. Barna reports improvement compared with a decade earlier. Do not infer clinical burnout, PTSD, a worsening trend, daily exhaustion, or a causal explanation from this figure.
- [Mark 6:30-34, NKJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+6%3A30-34&version=NKJV): returning apostles report; Jesus invites rest when demands leave no time to eat. The crowd then arrives and Jesus responds compassionately. The short visual paraphrases verses 30-32; preserve the fuller context in delivery. The pastoral-care application is ours, not a clinical recovery protocol.
- [Barna, July 6, 2026](https://www.barna.com/research/what-relieves-pastoral-burnout/): helpfulness/difficulty findings about extended rest, delegation and role alignment can support later delivery notes. Not an additional visual statistic or proof that those steps resolve every individual's distress.

Primary project foundation remains 2 Timothy 1:6-7 with verse 8 in the preserved talking sheet. Do not weaponize flame or power language as an instruction to push harder. Feelings are not a test of faith, and human capacity is not divine power. Privacy, consent, independent safe support, qualified care and urgent-help boundaries in v1 notes remain applicable. Do not ask a Pastor to diagnose themselves or disclose private history to church leaders.
