V6 preserves the three-page format and rebuilds the reader flow around James' directive that shared prayer is central to this work rather than an opening or closing formality.

## Content changes

- Smooths the opening statistics paragraph into recognition for the Pastor and a direct question for the church.
- Joins the Gethsemane material into a clearer progression from Jesus naming His sorrow to the Pastor's need for trustworthy relationships.
- Names prayer as part of those relationships: coming before the Father, listening to Jesus through His Word, and asking the Holy Spirit for truth, comfort, conviction, and direction.
- States plainly that the advisor, Pastor, and staff cannot manufacture real life change; God does that work.
- Simplifies the Paul and congregational-care transitions.
- Tightens all three reflection questions and makes shared prayer part of deciding what support or change is needed.
- Places prayer at three natural moments in the process: before evaluation, when difficult truth surfaces, and before deciding the next step.
