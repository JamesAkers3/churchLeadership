# Pastor Feedback Round: Rooted, Passion, and Formation Gap

## Purpose

James shared three PDFs with a pastor friend for an outside read. The comments were reviewed one visual at a time. Every original was preserved, each approved revision received all six QA perspectives, and the current working files now reflect the decisions below.

## Rooted

The pastor affirmed the page and questioned whether **Renewed** belonged in the three-part title. **Flourishing** more clearly matches the middle of the five-stage movement and gives the title a beginning, middle, and outward result.

Decision: **Rooted. Flourishing. Multiplying Life.** is the current title. The mature botanical panorama, seasons-not-scorecard guardrail, and Heartbeat remain unchanged.

## From Passion to Impact

The pastor affirmed the concept and identified two language inconsistencies:

1. Mission was described as both movement and direction without a clearly stated philosophy.
2. Passion was treated visually as the energy but verbally as something that creates the energy.

Decision:

- **Passion** is the energy we carry.
- **Vision** is a future picture that focuses us.
- **Mission** is faithful action that directs us.
- **Impact** is what happens where that action reaches people.

The Heartbeat now begins **Passion is the energy.** The lower path uses **Unclear Vision** and **Competing Missions**. The lower equation preserves James' plus/minus and multiply/divide metaphor while clarifying the relationship: **(Passion - Clear Vision) / Competing Missions = Limited Impact.** Each lower beam now meets and leaves its own reflector so the picture does not imply a serial mirror chain.

## The Formation Gap

The pastor affirmed the opening question and identified three needs:

1. Give the 41 and 9 percent findings equal space while clarifying who the 96 percent represents.
2. Make Jesus' formation pattern the unmistakable answer rather than a transition line.
3. Move the exhaustion warning into a quieter closing position and clarify the intended audience.

Decision: the page is for pastors and leadership teams together. All three findings now identify Protestant senior pastors and receive equal visual weight. The dominant answer is **Jesus called the Twelve to be with Him before He sent them out.** The page moves immediately into **With Him → Sent Together → Back With Him**, then asks one practical question and invites the leader to begin with one faithful believer already near them.

The closing acknowledges that formation may have begun late without leaving anyone in shame: **But it is not too late to begin.** James' protected Holy Spirit statement remains intact.

Current: `output/pdf/the-formation-gap-v2.pdf`

Preserved: `output/pdf/archive/the-formation-gap.pdf`

## QA lesson retained

Every reviewer must test the page as a complete communication, not only through a specialty lens. Each page must carry one coherent argument, lead to one clear next step, keep Jesus central to the practice itself, and avoid language that blames an exhausted pastor or pressures a church to manufacture a result.
