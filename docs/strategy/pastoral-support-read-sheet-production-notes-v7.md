# Pastoral-Support Read Sheet - Impact-Centered Review Candidate

**Created:** October 25, 2026

**Current review PDF:** `output/pdf/a-pastor-can-be-known-and-still-unsupported-read-sheet-v7.pdf`

**Editable master:** `docs/strategy/pastoral-support-read-sheet-v7.md`

V7 preserves the approved prayer-centered foundation and answers the October 25 four-lens QA finding that V6 was compassionate but emotionally restrained. It does not make the chapter harsher. It makes the center more truthful: a Pastor's strength can hide an unhealthy dependence, and appreciation is not support when the church's expectations remain unchanged.

## Reader-page changes

- Names the pastoral-support visual as the companion source of the Barna figures rather than referring to missing figures without context.
- Keeps “Who knows the soul beneath your role?” as the relational hinge.
- States that the stronger and more capable the Pastor, the longer an unhealthy imbalance can remain hidden.
- Strengthens Gethsemane without turning it into a leadership formula: no one could carry the cup for Jesus, He named His sorrow and prayed, the disciples fell asleep, and their failure did not make His honesty weakness or His request a mistake.
- Places shared prayer at the foundation: come before the Father, listen to Jesus through His Word, and ask the Holy Spirit to reveal truth, bring comfort and conviction, and lead faithful action.
- Reframes Exodus 17 around distinct participation and God's victory: Joshua fought, Aaron and Hur steadied Moses, and no one person was the whole work.
- Describes the Philippians' support as costly and practical rather than sentimental.
- Makes the central challenge direct: the Pastor's strength cannot become the church's plan; gratitude is not support when expectations remain unchanged.
- Requires a measurable response within 30 days: stop, fund, share, or delay something so the Pastor has less to carry.
- Moves the Andy Stanley quotation into facilitator material so it cannot visually compete with Scripture or become a public diagnosis of the Pastor.

## Facilitator companion

The PDF contains two reader pages followed by two clearly separated facilitator pages. The facilitator companion includes before, during and after safeguards; prayer prompts at the turning points; Scripture and content boundaries; supporting quotations; sources; and a prayer before choosing the next step.
