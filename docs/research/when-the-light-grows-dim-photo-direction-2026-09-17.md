## Design intent

The picture should convey that there is room to receive care and someone can be present with you. Avoid isolated silhouettes, head-in-hands distress, clinical role-play, forced cheerfulness, and dramatic darkness. This supports the existing Mark 6:30–32 Heartbeat without depicting stock people as Jesus or His disciples. It does not promise that companionship alone resolves exhaustion or trauma-related symptoms.
