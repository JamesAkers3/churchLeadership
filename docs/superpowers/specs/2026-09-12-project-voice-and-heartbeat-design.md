# Project Voice and Heartbeat

## What we are changing

We are rewriting the primary project documents so they sound like James. The language should be pastoral, direct, warm, and honest. It should feel like someone sitting beside a pastor, not speaking down to him or selling him a program.

The work includes:

- `PROJECT-CHECKPOINT.md`
- `docs/strategy/business-discovery-notes.md`
- `docs/research/jesus-three-twelve-leadership-research.md`
- `docs/strategy/when-the-light-grows-dim-framework.md`
- the three working visuals in `docs/strategy/assets`

Old brainstorming drafts and hidden working files are not part of this pass.

## What better means

Better does not mean bigger, busier, richer, or more impressive. It means healthier.

We are helping cultivate a healthy church—rooted in Christ, alive through the Holy Spirit, tended by the Father, and bearing fruit that reaches people with the saving grace of Jesus.

This is the foundation for every document and visual.

## The Heartbeat

Every framework and visual will have one clear Heartbeat moment. It will not become a required heading under every section.

Sometimes **The Heartbeat** will appear as a heading. Sometimes it will be carried by an opening sentence, a question, a Scripture passage, or a direct connection to the way Jesus led and loved people. The label should create a meaningful pause, not become a stamp.

The heartbeat must answer three questions:

- What did Jesus show us?
- What does that mean here?
- How should it shape what we do?

## Voice

### Scripture governs the work

James' correction, September 17, 2026: "I am not ok with paraphrasing Scriptures to make it fit our purpose, if it does not fit then remove the scripture reference all together."

This applies across the project, including visuals, worksheets, talking sheets and the mini-book. Quote Scripture exactly from a named translation, preserving its wording, punctuation and meaning. Clearly identify any faithful excerpt as an excerpt; never blend translations, substitute our wording or omit context that changes the meaning. Do not rewrite a passage to fit the available space or the point we want to make.

Our reflection and application must be clearly distinguished from the biblical text. They must not be presented as Scripture or use a reference to imply that our inference is an explicit biblical statement. Check each passage in context before using it. If it does not genuinely support the point, remove the reference and any misleading appeal to it rather than forcing a connection. Scripture guides and corrects the framework; the framework does not reshape Scripture.

Earlier artifacts stay preserved as history. Dim v1-v4 contain a custom summary labeled paraphrase and are not the corrected public-use candidates. On September 17 James approved replacing it with Jesus' exact spoken invitation from Mark 6:31, labeled NIV/Excerpt, in the new Dim v5 review candidate. All other wording remains unchanged; other PDFs and the talking sheet have not been corrected by that narrow revision. Details: `docs/strategy/when-the-light-grows-dim-production-notes-v5.md`.

The rewrite will use short, plain sentences. One thought at a time. It can be vivid, energetic, and full of conviction, but it should never feel polished past the point of sounding human.

We will remove consultant language, abstract labels, repeated contrasts, decorative explanations, and sentences that announce what the document is about instead of simply saying it.

We will not pretend to speak in Jesus' voice. We will point to His words, actions, compassion, questions, correction, restoration, and intentional way of forming people.

## What stays

### Pastor capitalization

Use **Pastor** and **Pastors** consistently in our own writing, including generic references and possessives. This is James' intentional editorial choice to honor the person and calling, not to place anyone above others. Keep direct quotations and published source titles as originally written. Preserve archived versions as historical records rather than revising them retroactively.

The rewrite will preserve:

- language James has approved and asked us to keep exactly;
- the meaning of each framework;
- biblical context and translation differences;
- source citations and necessary clinical guardrails;
- the approved passion-to-impact visual and its core language.

The research document will receive a lighter edit than the strategy documents. Accuracy comes first. The goal is to make it easier to hear and use without thinning out the study.

## File-by-file direction

The business notes will read like the foundation of a calling, not a consulting report.

The 1–3–12 study will keep its depth while sounding like a pastor carefully walking another leader through what Scripture shows.

The burnout framework will keep its safety boundaries while sounding like someone who understands the weight of ministry and knows how to sit with a hurting pastor.

The visuals will use fewer words, stronger labels, and one natural Heartbeat moment where it helps the viewer return to Christ.

The checkpoint will remain a useful record, but it will use plain language instead of process terminology.

## Done means

The pass is finished when every primary document has been reviewed line by line, all approved language remains intact, every major work is clearly rooted in Christ, and the project sounds like one person wrote it.
