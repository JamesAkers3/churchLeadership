CHRIST AT THE CENTER / 1-3-12 A relational pattern for shared ministry. Christ remains the source. Trusted people share the weight. Others are formed and entrusted to carry the mission. Jesus was intentional about proximity, formation and responsibility. C ABIDE IN THE SOURCE He holds the leader and the ministry. Remain in Him before leading others. 12 JOHN 15:5 3 1 1 STEWARD THE SOUL Lead from a life centered under Christ. Tend the person beneath the role. 1 TIMOTHY 4:16 CHRIST 3 BUILD TRUSTED SUPPORT Build mutual relationships with trusted peers or mentors who see the cost, pray, speak truth and help carry what should not be carried alone. MARK 14:33 / GALATIANS 6:2 12 RELATIONAL NEEDS, NOT A CHAIN OF COMMAND FORM AND ENTRUST Invite and equip people to learn, carry meaningful responsibility and grow according to their gifting and capacity. MARK 3:14-15 In the Gospels, the Three remained within the Twelve. In practice, a pastor's trusted support may include peers or mentors outside the team being led. Put names to the circles. Who helps you remain rooted? Who can see the cost? Who are you forming and entrusting? Start with one name and one faithful step. If a circle is empty, do not fill it quickly. Begin prayerfully with one relationship. A RELATIONAL FRAMEWORK, NOT A REQUIRED HEADCOUNT. DIFFERENT PROXIMITY DOES NOT MEAN DIFFERENT VALUE—AND MUST NEVER MEAN LESS ACCOUNTABILITY. Scripture: John 15:5; 1 Timothy 4:16; Mark 3:14-15; 5:37; 9:2; 14:33; Galatians 6:2. This framework applies patterns seen in Jesus' relationships; it is not an organizational formula commanded in Scripture.
