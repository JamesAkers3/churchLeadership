From passion to impact

Focused light changes what it reaches.

Passion creates the energy. Vision brings it into focus. Mission points it somewhere that matters. Impact happens where it lands.

Aligned
PASSION + VISION × MISSION = EXTRAVAGANT RESULTS
PassionEnergy
VisionClear focus
MissionOne direction
ImpactLife takes root
Fractured
PASSION − VISION ÷ MISSION = NO IMPACT
PassionStill burning
VisionDistorted
MissionsCompeting
ImpactNever lands
Distorted vision corrupts the focus. Competing missions divide the direction.
The Heartbeat: Jesus knew why He came. He prayed, chose where to go next, and would not let the crowd choose His mission. See Mark 1:35–39.
