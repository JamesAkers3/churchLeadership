Working transformation metaphor

From Rooted Passion to Multiplying Impact

A church is not “dead” because it looks bare. The calling and roots may still be alive beneath the surface. Focus, clarity, and life-giving systems help that latent passion become visible fruit—and eventually life beyond itself.

1

Rooted

The calling and passion remain, even when visible growth has stalled.

2

Focused

Vision and mission align scattered energy. Flashlight becomes laser.

3

Flourishing

Life-giving systems nourish people and create capacity for ministry.

4

Fruitful

Authentic community relationships produce visible, shared impact.

5

Multiplying

Seeds travel beyond the church; new life takes root around it.

Why the biology strengthens the story

  • Roots represent calling, theology, values, and spiritual health.
  • Buds represent renewed clarity and focused possibility.
  • Flowers and pollinators represent relationships and mutual exchange with the community.
  • Fruit and seeds represent life change that can travel and reproduce.
  • Saplings represent impact that no longer depends entirely on the original church.

Pastoral guardrail

Call the first stage rooted or dormant, never dead. A struggling church may still have deep faith, faithful people, and living potential. Your role is to help reveal and focus that life—not pronounce judgment on it.
